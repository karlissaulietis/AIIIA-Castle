# AIIIA Castle 👑

### Learn • Evolve • Play to Earn

**AIIIA Castle** is an independent, AI-powered Telegram Mini App combining intelligent companions, personalized conversations, digital art, interactive experiences, and a gamified Diamond reward ecosystem.

## Explore the Castle

- 👑 **Amara** — An AI companion with a distinctive, commanding personality, offering immersive conversations and consensual roleplay.
- ✨ **Stacey** — An AI assistant supporting productivity, organization, and everyday tasks.
- 🎨 **Digital Art Gallery** — A creative space for artists, collectors, and community participation.
- 💎 **Diamond Rewards** — A gamified system built around participation, activities, and community engagement.
- 🏰 **Personal Experiences** — Conversations, diaries, challenges, and evolving AI interactions.
- 💙 **TON Blockchain** — Live support for voluntary TON tributes, authorized through users' own wallets and verified on-chain.

## Our Vision

To build an evolving digital world where artificial intelligence, creativity, entertainment, and community come together.
